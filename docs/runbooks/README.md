# Operational runbooks

## Purpose

Repeatable procedures for developing, validating and operating the backend locally.

## Responsibilities

Turns environment and operational knowledge into steps another contributor can execute safely.

## What belongs here

- Local startup and shutdown.
- Database and migration procedures.
- Troubleshooting and recovery steps.
- Future deployment or incident procedures.

## What does not belong here

- Business requirements.
- Unverified personal workarounds.
- Credentials.
